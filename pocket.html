Your Pocket Companion is ready

Twelve pages that fit on the fridge door: the first-minute checks, the water count, the cold-chain rules for food, baby feeds and medicines, and the contacts sheet. Print it, fill it in, and keep it where everyone can find it in the dark.

Download the PDF

The Pocket Companion is a 12-page A5 PDF, about 60 KB, designed to be printed double-sided on three sheets of A4 or read on a phone.

Download the Pocket Companion (PDF)

Review edition. The Pocket Companion is being checked alongside the handbook and this file will be replaced as the review progresses; if you are on the list, we will send you the updated version. If the download does not start, open the PDF in your browser and save it from there.

What to do with it

  1. Print it and walk through the first-minute page with everyone in the house, including children who are old enough to turn off a tap.
  2. Fill in the contacts sheet and the medicines list now, while nothing is wrong.
  3. Put one copy on the fridge and one in the bag or drawer you would reach for first.

What happens next

If you joined the list, you will receive a confirmation email first; please click the link in it so we are allowed to write to you. After that you will hear from us when the full handbook launches, and occasionally when a source in the evidence register changes in a way that matters.

Did not mean to sign up? Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and our privacy notice explains what we hold and how to have it deleted.
