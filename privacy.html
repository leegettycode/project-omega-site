Privacy notice

Last updated 9 November 2026. This notice explains what personal data Project Omega collects through this website, why, and what your rights are under UK data protection law.

Who we are

Project Omega is an independent book publisher based in the United Kingdom and is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. You can contact us at hello@example.com.

What we collect

When you ask for the Pocket Companion or join our list we collect your email address. Our email provider also records the date and time you subscribed, the fact that you confirmed your subscription, and a tag showing that you came from this website. We do not ask for your name, address or any other personal information, and this website does not set cookies or run analytics.

If you email us, we keep your message and our reply for as long as needed to deal with it.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

We use your email address to send you the Pocket Companion, to tell you when The First 72 Hours launches, and to send occasional news about the handbook and any later books in the series, including corrections when a source we rely on changes. We do this on the basis of your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you give by entering your address and confirming it from the email we send you. You can withdraw consent at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If you unsubscribe, our provider keeps a minimal suppression record so we do not email you again by mistake. We review the list at least once a year and remove addresses that have not confirmed their subscription.

Who we share it with

Your address is stored and processed by our email list provider, which acts as our processor and may not use it for any other purpose. Our provider may be based outside the United Kingdom, in which case transfers are protected by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or by a UK adequacy decision. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs standard technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes under GitHub's own privacy statement.

We do not sell, rent or otherwise share your address with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising on other platforms.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to our processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time, which you can do by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing us.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@example.com. We will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or by telephone on 0303 123 1113.

Children

Our list is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect the email addresses of anyone under 13. If you believe a child has subscribed, email us and we will remove the address.

Changes to this notice

If we change the way we use your data we will update this page and, where the change is significant, email you before it takes effect.
